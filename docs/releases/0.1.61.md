# EverythingMac 0.1.61

## Changes

- Name searches match only the files currently in the index and check them in parallel across processor cores. Previously every search checked, one at a time, every name the app had seen since it started, including names of files that no longer exist. Results and their order are unchanged.
- Folder-scoped searches collect matching names with less work.

## Performance

Measured on a 4.57-million-entry index, including sorting and the first 128 results:

- Typing `a` (2.6 million matches, sorted by name) took 188 ms and now takes 10.5 ms.
- Small searches such as `everything-mac` took 26 ms and now take about 3 ms; `.js`, `.py`, and `.swift` searches fell from 34–49 ms to 4–7 ms.
- A search for `swift` within `/Users/` fell from 66 ms to 29 ms. Folder-scoped searches gain less because most of their time is spent listing the folder.
- Exact and prefix lookups, the all-files view, and index loading are unchanged.

For fourteen queries, including the complete 2.6-million-result lists, every result and its position matched 0.1.60.

## Validation

- Passed 1,824 Rust tests, with the system-wide search cancellation test excluded, and clippy without warnings.
- Passed the saved-index, sort, selection, feature, live, tab, and terminal checks. `--trash-check` times out at step 23, as it does on 0.1.60.
