# EverythingMac 0.1.66

## Changes

- Searching, scrolling, and file actions keep working during a rescan. The current index stays in use until the rebuilt one replaces it, instead of the app waiting for the whole rescan.
- Large folders that appear or change on disk, such as a big folder moved into place, are read in the background. Searches continue meanwhile, the status shows Updating, and the results update when reading finishes.
- The index now covers the startup disk only. Other volumes inside the monitored root are skipped: external and network drives, disk images, Xcode Simulator runtimes, system volumes such as Preboot and Recovery, and /dev. To search one, add its mount point or a folder on it to Include paths. Mounting a drive no longer makes the app stop responding while it reads the whole drive.
- Indexes saved by earlier versions drop their entries from other volumes on the next launch, without a rescan.

## Performance

- During a 14-second rescan of a 4.3-million-entry index, 188 searches were drawn in a median 60 ms, against 55 ms with no rescan, and pages far down the results loaded in about 5 ms. Earlier versions ran neither until the rescan finished.
- Moving a 200,000-file folder into the index held up searches for at most 132–136 ms, down from 666–799 ms.
- On the test machine, skipping other volumes removed 834,966 of 5,034,016 entries, most of them from an Xcode Simulator runtime.

## Validation

- Passed 1,837 Rust tests, with the system-wide search cancellation test excluded, and clippy without warnings.
- Passed the saved-index, sort, selection, feature, live, tab, terminal, and Trash checks, and a new rescan check.
- A disk image mounted inside a watched folder stayed out of the index during live updates and full scans, and was indexed when its mount point was included.
